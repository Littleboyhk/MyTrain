Features

Everything you need for the platform.

Built for frequent Indian Railways travelers who want accurate live tracking and quick PNR checks, wrapped in a calm, glassy interface.

Real-time tracking

An animated station timeline with running status, delay chips, and a live position that updates as the train moves.

Crowd-verified position

Anonymous GPS pings from riders on the same train are combined into a single median position, resistant to outliers, so you get a fix even where official data is stale.

Instant PNR status

Chart status plus a per-passenger breakdown — Confirmed, RAC, or Waitlisted — with a clear booking-to-current comparison.

Privacy-first by design

Location sharing is opt-in only, tied to rotating anonymous IDs, auto-stops when you leave the train, and raw pings are deleted after 48 hours.

Search your way

Find a train by route (origin to destination and date) or jump straight in by train number, with fast type-ahead suggestions.

Designed for the dark

A calm Liquid Glass interface with light and dark modes, smooth motion, and haptics, tuned to stay readable on a moving train.

How it works

Two layers of data, one live dot.

The app talks only to our backend, never third-party APIs directly, then fuses two sources into the most accurate position available.

01

Search & open

Pick your train by route or number. We surface its baseline running status, cached and refreshed only for trains people are actually tracking.

02

See it live

The tracking screen shows the timeline and a position marker labelled Estimated or Crowd-verified depending on how fresh the data is.

03

Contribute (optional)

On the train? Tap to share your location anonymously. Your pings help everyone else on board, then vanish after 48 hours.

PNR status

Your whole ticket, at a glance.

Enter a 10-digit PNR to see the train, route, chart status, and every passenger's confirmation, colour-coded so you can read it in a second.

  • Chart prepared / not prepared, made obvious
  • Confirmed, RAC and Waitlisted, colour-coded
  • Booking-to-current upgrades highlighted

FAQ

Good questions.

When is My Train launching?

It's in active development. Join the waitlist above and you'll be notified the moment early access opens.

Is my location private?

Yes. Sharing is strictly opt-in, uses rotating anonymous IDs that aren't tied to your identity, stops automatically when you leave the train, and raw location pings are deleted after 48 hours.

Which trains and stations are supported?

The app covers Indian Railways with a catalog of 8,989 stations. Live accuracy improves as more passengers on a given train contribute positions.

Will it cost anything?

Pricing isn't decided yet. Waitlist members will hear first.
